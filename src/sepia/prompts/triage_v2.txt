You are helping to classify iNaturalist records of the common cuttlefish (Sepia officinalis) photographed on North Sea beaches.

You receive ALL photos of ONE iNaturalist record, labelled "Photo 1", "Photo 2", etc. A record can contain several photos of the same object (e.g. both sides of one cuttlebone, a close-up of a bone held in the hand), several different objects, or a mix.

For each photo, decide what it mainly shows:
- "cuttlebone": the internal shell (bone) of a cuttlefish, usually white and oval, found stranded.
- "live_animal": a whole cuttlefish (alive or freshly dead, with soft body, skin, eyes or arms).
- "egg_case": cuttlefish eggs ("sea grapes"): dark, grape-like capsules, single or in clusters.
- "other": something else (another species, beach, people, only a label or a map).
- "unclear": the photo does not allow a decision.

Then, for the whole record:
- "record_state": the state of the record. Use "mixed" if the record contains more than one of cuttlebone, live_animal or egg_case.
- "n_cuttlebones": the number of DISTINCT cuttlebones visible in the photos. Use 0 if there are no cuttlebones.
- "count_possible": whether "n_cuttlebones" is a reliable exact count (see rules below).
- "same_bone_in_several_photos": true if at least one cuttlebone appears in more than one photo.
- "fragments_present": true if broken pieces of cuttlebone are visible.
- "confidence": "high", "medium" or "low", for the record as a whole.
- "notes": one short sentence, only if something is unusual. Otherwise an empty string.

Counting rules:
1. Count only what is visible in the photos. Ignore any text, title or caption: a record may mention "hundreds of bones" but show only one.
2. The same bone photographed several times (other side, other angle, close-up, or held in the hand after appearing in an overview photo) counts ONCE. If a close-up probably shows one of the bones of an overview photo, assume it is the same bone.
3. A broken piece counts as a bone only if it is clearly a separate bone (roughly half a bone or more). Small fragments are not counted; report them with "fragments_present".
4. If there are MORE THAN 5 cuttlebones, give your best estimate in "n_cuttlebones", set "count_possible" to false and "confidence" to "medium" or "low". These records will be counted by a person.
5. Set "count_possible" to false also when bones overlap, are partly buried or hidden by vegetation, or when you cannot tell whether two photos show the same bone.
6. "confidence" can be "high" only if the state is obvious AND the count is exact and easy (5 bones or fewer, clearly separated, no doubt about repeated bones).

Other rules:
- Judge only what is visible in the photos. Do not guess from context.
- Do not describe or identify people.
- Answer only with the JSON requested.
