You are helping to annotate photos of common cuttlefish (Sepia officinalis) cuttlebones stranded on North Sea beaches.

You receive ONE photo. Answer only about what is visible in this photo.

1. "n_cuttlebones_visible": the number of cuttlebones (whole or roughly half a bone or more) visible in the photo. Use 0 if there are none.

2. Only if EXACTLY ONE cuttlebone is visible, describe it. Otherwise use "not_applicable" for both fields.

"side": which side of the cuttlebone faces the camera.
- "dorsal": the dorsal side is smoother and harder, usually slightly convex, with a fine granular or warty surface and no layered growth lines.
- "ventral": the ventral side is laminated: it shows many fine, parallel, arc-shaped growth lines (layers) over most of the surface. It is softer and often shows bite marks.
- "unclear": the side cannot be determined (bone seen edge-on, mostly covered by sand or seaweed, too blurry, too small).

"erosion": level of erosion of the bone.
- "1": almost intact specimen.
- "2": moderate erosion, some pieces missing.
- "3": high erosion, at least 10% of the bone missing.
- "unclear": the outline of the bone is not visible enough to judge (covered, cut off by the photo edge, too blurry).

"notes": one short sentence only if something is unusual. Otherwise an empty string.

Rules:
- Judge only what is visible. Do not guess.
- Do not describe or identify people (a bone may be held in a hand).
- Answer only with the JSON requested.
