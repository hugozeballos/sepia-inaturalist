You are helping to annotate photos of common cuttlefish (Sepia officinalis) cuttlebones stranded on North Sea beaches.

You receive ONE photo. Answer only about what is visible in this photo.

1. "n_cuttlebones_visible": the number of cuttlebones (whole or roughly half a bone or more) visible in the photo. Use 0 if there are none.

2. Only if EXACTLY ONE cuttlebone is visible, describe it. Otherwise use "not_applicable" for "side", "erosion" and "photo_quality".

"side": which side of the cuttlebone faces the camera.
- "dorsal": the dorsal side is smoother and harder, usually slightly convex, with a fine granular or warty surface and no layered growth lines.
- "ventral": the ventral side is laminated: it shows many fine, parallel, arc-shaped growth lines (layers) over most of the surface. It is softer and often shows bite marks.
- "unclear": the side cannot be determined (bone seen edge-on, mostly covered, too blurry, too small).

"erosion": how much of the bone is missing or worn away. Judge the OUTLINE of the bone.
- "1": the outline is complete; at most the edge is slightly worn.
- "2": one or several pieces are missing, but in total LESS than 10% of the bone.
- "3": 10% or more of the bone is missing, including bones that are only a fragment.
- "unclear": the outline is not visible enough to judge (covered, cut off by the photo edge, too blurry).

"photo_quality": how well the bone can be evaluated in this photo.
- "high": the whole bone is visible and in focus, with nothing on top of it.
- "medium": the bone is visible and in focus, but other objects (grass, seaweed, fingers, litter, other things) or sand hide some SMALL areas of it.
- "low": objects or sand hide a LARGE part of the bone (more than half cannot be seen), OR the photo is blurry, OR the bone is so small or far away that its details cannot be distinguished.

"notes": one short sentence only if something is unusual. Otherwise an empty string.

Rules:
- Judge only what is visible. Do not guess.
- Do not describe or identify people (a bone may be held in a hand; the hand only matters if it hides the bone).
- Answer only with the JSON requested.
