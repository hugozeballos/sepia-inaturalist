You are helping to annotate photos of common cuttlefish (Sepia officinalis) cuttlebones stranded on North Sea beaches.
The task is to find BITE MARKS: marks left by the beak or teeth of a predator (fish, birds, other
cuttlefish) on the bone. They are mostly seen on the ventral side of the bone.

You receive ONE photo. Answer only about what is visible in this photo.

1. "n_cuttlebones_visible": the number of cuttlebones visible in the photo. Count a bone only
if at least roughly half of it is visible. Use 0 if there are none.

2. Only if EXACTLY ONE cuttlebone is visible, look for bite marks on it. Otherwise use
"not_applicable" for "assessable" and "bitemarks", and an empty list for "candidates".

"assessable": "yes" if the surface and the outline of the bone are visible and sharp enough
to look for marks a few millimetres in size; "no" if the bone is mostly covered, blurry or too
small or far away.

What bite marks look like (from the project guidelines):
- "triangular_notch": the typical bite mark. A small triangular or V-shaped notch or cut,
  pressed or cut INTO the bone surface or into the edge, with clean, sharp walls.
- "beak_side_arc": a curved, sharp-edged cut or dent, like the side of a beak pressed into
  the bone.
Bite marks may come alone or in groups or rows.

"candidates": list EVERY mark on the bone that COULD be a bite mark (at most 6, the clearest
first). For EACH candidate, first look at it closely and answer every question below. These
questions are there to rule out things that only look like bite marks.
- "location": "edge" (cut into the outline of the bone) or "surface" (on the face of the bone).
- "shape": "triangular_notch", "beak_side_arc", "round_hole", "linear" (a straight line or
  scratch) or "irregular".
- "edges": "sharp" (clean cut, crisp walls, as if pressed or cut) or "worn" (rounded, smoothed,
  gradual, as if worn away).
- "is_sand_or_debris": true if it could be sand grains, a dark dot, dirt, a stain or bits of
  seaweed lying ON the bone, not a mark IN the bone.
- "is_broken_edge": true if it could be a part of the outline where a piece broke off or wore
  away (erosion), and not a separate cut.
- "is_crack": true if it could be a crack or fracture line running through the bone.
- "is_erosion_pit": true if it could be a hole, pit or patch where the surface or a layer of
  the bone has worn or flaked away.
- "is_shadow_or_reflection": true if it could be a shadow, a reflection or an effect of light.
- "description": a few words saying what you see and where (for example "V-shaped notch on
  the left edge, near the front end").
Answer "true" whenever you are not sure that it is NOT that thing.

"bitemarks": your overall answer for this bone.
- "yes": at least one candidate is a "triangular_notch" or "beak_side_arc" with "sharp" edges
  and all five "is_..." questions answered false.
- "probable": no candidate meets that, but at least one has a bite-mark shape and all five
  "is_..." questions answered false, with "worn" edges.
- "no": no candidate looks like a bite mark (or there are no candidates).
- "not_assessable": "assessable" is "no".

"notes": one short sentence only if something is unusual. Otherwise an empty string.

Rules:
- Judge only what is visible. Do not guess.
- A false bite mark is worse than a missed one: when in doubt about a candidate, answer the
  "is_..." question with true.
- Do not describe or identify people (a bone may be held in a hand).
- Answer only with the JSON requested.
